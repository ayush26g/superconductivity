### Procedure
1. Select the superconductor material (e.g., YBCO with $T_c = 93\text{ K}$) from the dropdown menu.
2. Keep the external magnetic field enabled to monitor flux line interactions.
3. Note that at room temperature ($300\text{ K}$), the material possesses normal electrical resistance and the magnet rests directly on the disc.
4. Click **Pour Liquid Nitrogen (Cool to 77K)** or drag the temperature slider below the material's critical temperature ($T_c$).
5. Observe the flux lines bending around the disc and the magnet levitating into the air.
6. Check the $R\text{ vs }T$ graph to verify that resistance has collapsed to $0\,\Omega$.
