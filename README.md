<a name="LO"></a>
#### 1. Focus Area : Reinforce theoretical concept.
Demonstrate zero electrical resistance below critical temperature = 93 K and magnetic flux exclusion (Meissner effect) in high-T_c ceramic YBCO using interactive 3D WebXR simulation.

#### 2. Learning Objectives and Cognitive Level


Sr. No |	Learning Objective	| Cognitive Level | Action Verb
:--|:--|:--|:-:
1.| Recall the definition of superconductivity and state the critical transition temperature for YBCO  <br>   |  Remembering  |   Recall,State
2.| Explain how a superconductor differs from a hypothetical perfect conductor through magnetic flux expulsion.  <br>   | Understanding   |   Analyze, Correlate
3.| Evaluate the physical principles of flux pinning and magnetic levitation stability that overcome Earnshaw's theorem.  <br>   | Evaluating   |   Evaluate, Justify

<a name="IS"></a>
#### 3. Instructional Strategy
###### Name of Instructional Strategy  : Inquiry-Based Active Learning & Virtual Laboratory Simulation
Assessment Method : Diagnostic Pre-test evaluation, Real-time interactive telemetry tracking, and Evaluative Post-test assessment.   <u>   
###### Assessment Method:   Diagnostic Pre-test evaluation, Real-time interactive telemetry tracking, and Evaluative Post-test assessment.

<u> <b>Description: Students begin by reviewing theoretical concepts and answering a diagnostic pre-test. They next enter the 3D WebXR virtual laboratory where they adjust the temperature of a YBCO pellet using cryogenic presets (77 K liquid nitrogen) and manual stepping buttons . Students observe the dynamic R–T curve drop to  T_c = 93 K, toggle 3D magnetic field lines to witness Meissner flux expulsion, and analyze the resulting magnetic levitation before completing an evaluative post-test. </b>    </u>
